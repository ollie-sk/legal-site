Privacy Policy

Ollie (“the app”) is a skate trick practice log for iPhone. This policy explains what stays on your device and when the app asks for permission. Contact: ollie.skate.help@gmail.com.

Information the app keeps

Trick names, categories, spot notes, attempt results, key points, reel files, and pass balance are stored in the app’s private storage on this device. Ollie does not create an account and does not upload that practice record to an Ollie server.

If you film an attempt, the camera captures video on the device. If you import or save a reel, the app reads or writes photos and videos in your photo library only after you allow it. If you record a voice note or use speech input, the microphone captures audio and the speech recognizer turns that voice into text on the device. Those photo, photos, audio, voice, and device items are used only to build your trick log and reel.

Permission

Camera, photo library, microphone, speech recognition, and notifications are requested when you use the related control, or, for notifications, the first time you open the trick list. You can deny any request. Denying camera still lets you import from Photos. Denying the microphone still lets you type notes. You agree to each system permission by choosing Allow on Apple’s prompt. If you do not allow a permission, that feature stays unavailable and the rest of the app continues.

Purchases

Reel exports use consumable passes sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. Ollie stores the resulting pass balance on this device. Removing the app or clearing its data removes that balance. There is no restore action because these are consumable passes.

Third parties

Ollie does not sell your practice record and does not send your photos, voice, or notes to a third-party content service. Apple provides the device, the App Store, and the system permission dialogs. A third party does not receive your skate clips for advertising.

Children

Ollie is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the app to collect information about a child.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated page will be published at this same address. Continued use of the app after the update means you accept the revised policy. See also the Terms of Use.
